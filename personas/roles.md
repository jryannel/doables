## Open role questions
- Is "Buyer" a meaningful function at all while Doables has no visible pricing? Today the buyer is whoever picks the tool and creates the first list; if a paid tier appears, SMB owners or agency leads may split off as a separate buyer role. → [SIG-xxx]
- The list organizer was split into team lead and household organizer (see change history). Open: is the split right, i.e. do motivation (delivery vs. fairness of chores) and authority (can assign work vs. no formal power) really differ? Do club coordinators fit with household organizers, or with team leads? → [SIG-xxx]
- In teams of 2–20, does the business owner act as a separate approver, or is the owner usually the organizer themselves (approval and adoption in one person)? Does the no-email/no-password identity model trigger a GDPR or data-location check that only the owner can clear? → [SIG-xxx]
- Does the office manager exist as a separate administrator in small offices, or is administration (members, sign-in keys, lost access) always done by the list organizer? → [SIG-xxx]
- Is the field worker in trades a distinct user role (mobile only, on site, gloves, poor connectivity, low tool affinity) or a segment variant of the task contributor? → [SIG-xxx]
- Do agency clients or club members actually look at shared lists without doing tasks (recipient role), or do they only receive updates through chat/email from the organizer? → [SIG-xxx]
- In clubs and associations, does the board approve tools, or do volunteers adopt them bottom-up without any approval? → [SIG-xxx]
- In freelancer collectives there is no hierarchy: does the organizer role rotate per project, making "organizer" a situation rather than a role? → [SIG-xxx]
