# FLOW-002: Join a shared list and get my tasks done

Role(s): task-contributor | Job: find out what I'm supposed to do, do it, and let the others know it's done | Stories: – | Issues: –

## Trigger
A colleague (or flatmate, or club member) says in the group chat: "I put this week's tasks into Doables – please have a look and tick off yours." You have never used Doables.

## Steps
1. Open Doables for the first time and get started.
2. Find out how to get to the shared list (there is no invite link in this test – see what the app offers for joining someone else's list).
3. Find the tasks that are yours.
4. Look at what's due today.
5. Add a comment or a question to a task.
6. Mark a task as done.
7. Come back later: find out how you'd get back in on your phone.

## Outcome
You know what's yours, you've done one thing, and the others can see it.

## Errors and edge cases
- You never received a proper invitation.
- You use a second device.
