# FLOW-001: Plan the coming week together

Role(s): team-lead, household-organizer | Job: split this week's work among the people involved and keep track of it | Stories: – | Issues: –

## Trigger
It's Monday morning (or Sunday evening at home). Several things have to get done this week by different people, and right now they are scattered across chat messages and heads.

## Steps
1. Open Doables for the first time and get started.
2. Create a list for this week.
3. Add 5–8 tasks, some with a due date and a short description.
4. Assign tasks to the people who should do them (or find out how).
5. Share the list with the others so they can see and finish their tasks.
6. Check what is due today and what is still open.
7. Mark one task as done.

## Outcome
Everyone involved can see the week's tasks, who does what, and what is due when.

## Errors and edge cases
- Some people you want to assign aren't in Doables yet.
- You come back later on another device.
