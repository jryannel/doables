# Research: Competitors and alternatives

Scope: tools named in intent.md plus specialized household, club, and trades tools that serve the same jobs.
All accessed 2026-09-26. "Fetched" = page opened and read; "search result" = seen in a web search result summary only (lower confidence, verify before relying on details).

## Doables (own product, reference)
- Onboarding asks only for a display name ("What should we call you?"); the name is shown next to tasks a person adds and finishes in shared lists. Returning users on another device use a "sign in" link. No pricing visible on the welcome page.
  Source: https://doables.gelbkappe.de (fetched, 2026-09-26)

## Microsoft To Do
- Shared lists via "Create invitation link", link pasted into email or messenger.
  Source: https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/todo/share-a-task-list (fetched, 2026-09-26)
- Sharing is supported only between personal Microsoft accounts, or between accounts within the same place of work or education (no cross-organization sharing).
  Source: https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/todo/create-and-share-lists (fetched, 2026-09-26)
- Both owner and recipient need an Exchange Online-backed account (Microsoft 365 or Outlook.com); otherwise sharing fails with an error.
  Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/answers/questions/5525689/microsoft-to-do-list-cannot-be-shared-to-other-mic (fetched, 2026-09-26)
- Tasks can be assigned to any member of a shared list; assigned tasks appear in an "Assigned to you" smart list; moving a task to another list removes the assignment.
  Source: https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/assign-tasks-in-shared-lists-1b12ff41-f204-4a2c-975d-edbf631a4b34 (fetched, 2026-09-26)
- User complaint: assigning requires first setting up a shared list and waiting for the other person to join; reminders can only be set for oneself, even on tasks assigned to others.
  Source: https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/discussions/to-do/to-do-task-assignment-feature-is-inconvenient--unable-to-set-reminders-for-tasks/4028520 (fetched, 2026-09-26)
- Several community threads about failed joins ("Unable to access a Microsoft To Do list invitation", "TO DO app: can't join list", "Why can't anyone join my To Do lists? Error 'List Isn't Available'").
  Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/answers/questions/5975091/unable-to-access-a-microsoft-to-do-list-invitation , https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/answers/questions/5688092/to-do-app-cant-join-list , https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/answers/questions/5701701/why-cant-anyone-join-my-to-do-lists-error-list-isn (titles seen in search results, 2026-09-26)
- Only one assignee per task.
  Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/answers/questions/1292126/assign-same-task-to-two-or-more-people-microsoft-t (search result, 2026-09-26)

## Todoist
- Collaboration limits: 5 people per personal project, 250 per team project; Beginner (free) plan 5 active personal projects; Pro 300 personal projects; Business up to 500 team projects.
  Source: https://www.todoist.com/help/articles/usage-limits-in-todoist-e5rcSY (fetched, 2026-09-26)
- Collaborators are invited by name or email address via "Share"; tasks can be assigned in personal and team projects.
  Source: https://www.todoist.com/help/articles/collaborate-in-todoist-WOpFVjup7 (fetched, 2026-09-26)
- Only one person can be assigned per task ("no doubt about who's responsible").
  Source: https://www.todoist.com/help/articles/manage-team-tasks-in-todoist-S99543QzY (search result, 2026-09-26)

## Google Tasks
- Shared tasks exist only when created in a Google Chat space or (on eligible Workspace plans) in Google Docs; lists themselves are not shared. Assigned tasks appear in the assignee's personal list; shared tasks cannot have subtasks or repeat.
  Source: https://support.google.com/tasks/answer/11549608?hl=en (fetched, 2026-09-26)

## Apple Reminders
- Shared iCloud reminder lists; reminders can be assigned only to people the list is shared with; assignee receives a notification.
  Source: https://support.apple.com/guide/reminders/assign-shared-reminders-remnf1ce7672/mac and https://support.apple.com/en-us/105124 (search results, 2026-09-26)

## Trello
- Free Workspaces are limited to 10 collaborators (members, guests, and pending invites count). Above the limit, boards become view-only, no new collaborators can be added, automations stop.
  Source: https://support.atlassian.com/trello/docs/workspace-user-limit/ (fetched, 2026-09-26)

## Asana
- Free Personal plan: up to 2 users (price data verified by the site 2026-08-19).
  Source: https://costbench.com/software/project-management/asana/free-plan/ (fetched, 2026-09-26)
- Reported change from 10 to 2 users, effective November 2025.
  Source: https://www.rock.so/blog/asana-pricing and https://aivario.com/tools/asana (search results, 2026-09-26)

## Cozi (households)
- Shared to-do and chore lists for the family, members assignable to lists, recurring chores (Cozi Chores), real-time sync.
  Source: https://www.cozi.com/to-do-lists/ (search result, 2026-09-26)
- Free version with ads; free calendar limited to a 30-day window.
  Source: https://www.usecalendara.com/blog/cozi-review-2026 (search result, 2026-09-26)

## Flatastic (flat shares, DACH)
- Modules: cleaning schedule with score system and automated reminders ("everyone sees who has to do what and how much each person has done"), shared shopping lists, expenses with who-owes-whom, pinboard, web app. "Free to download"; pricing not stated.
  Source: https://www.flatastic-app.com/en/ (fetched, 2026-09-26)
- Cleaning schedule: set deadlines, order, and frequency; a task assigned to one flatmate can be checked off by another.
  Source: https://www.appgefahren.de/flatastic-app-fuer-wgs-nach-update-mit-ueberarbeiteter-pinnwand-putzplan-und-finanz-bereich-171954.html (search result, 2026-09-26)

## Couple/mental-load apps
- fiftyfifty positions on making "the invisible burden of mental load and care work visible" and distributing it fairly.
  Source: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6749406018 (search result, 2026-09-26)
- ChoreLoop and Chore Wars position on effort-based points / a "fairer split of the mental load".
  Source: https://apps.apple.com/de/app/choreloop/id6757499941 , https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6759489229 (search results, 2026-09-26)

## Spond (clubs)
- Free club/team app; events can carry helper tasks (e.g. driver, referee); some tasks assigned to specific people, others open for sign-up and to be confirmed or declined.
  Source: https://www.spond.com/de/ and http://www.taekwondo-bautzen.de/uploads/file/Dokumente/Anleitung-App-SPOND.pdf (search results, 2026-09-26)

## No-account shared list apps (direct positioning overlap)
- Shared Lists (web): "No account needed", "Share with anyone – they don't need an account", "No personal data stored – fully anonymous". No task assignment or notifications described.
  Source: https://shared-lists.com/en/todo-list (fetched, 2026-09-26)
- App Store apps with no-sign-up sharing: "ToDo - Check and Share", "tDo", "ListShare" (guest mode, share via single link).
  Source: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6747980339 , https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id1609928725 , https://apps.apple.com/app/listshare-app/id6759029900 (search results, 2026-09-26)

## Trades planning software (SMB trades)
- Digital "Plantafel" and task apps for trades: MeinHandwerker, openHandwerk, Craftboxx, pds, clockin. Worker completes the task, adds a comment/photo, marks done; feedback appears in the office system without a call.
  Source: https://mein-handwerker-app.de/aufgabenverwaltung/ and https://openhandwerk.de/blog/einsatzplanung-mit-kalenderansicht-und-drag-and-drop-die-digitale-plantafel-revolutioniert-das-handwerk/ (search results, 2026-09-26)
