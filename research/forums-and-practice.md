All accessed 2026-09-26.

## Messenger use (DACH)
- 91 % of German craft businesses use messengers (WhatsApp, Signal, Telegram) for internal communication (Bitkom/ZDH, 503 businesses, representative telephone survey, published 2022-07-01).
  Source: https://www.bitkom.org/Presse/Presseinformation/Handwerk-wird-digitaler (fetched, 2026-09-26)
- Bitkom 2025 trades study: 91 % internal and 63 % external messenger use; 76 % of businesses say employees need more digital competence (2022: 56 %); 58 % see training needs as a barrier; 504 businesses.
  Source: https://www.bitkom.org/sites/main/files/2026-01/bitkom-studienbericht-handwerk.pdf (search result, 2026-09-26)
- WhatsApp is used by 81 % of internet users in Germany.
  Source: https://www.bitkom.org/Presse/Presseinformation/Eine-Milliarde-Kurznachrichten-pro-Tag (search result, 2026-09-26)

## Tasks in chat (SMB)
- In group chats, task instructions disappear in long threads, responsibilities are implied rather than assigned, and chat cannot show what is pending or overdue.
  Source: https://flowhubr.com/blog/project-management/why-using-whatsapp-to-manage-internal-tasks-fails-for-smes/ (search result, 2026-09-26; page body could not be fetched)

## Clubs and volunteers
- Managing volunteers through WhatsApp groups, spreadsheets, or email threads is "time-consuming, unreliable, and frustrating for everyone involved"; WhatsApp mixes club data with volunteers' personal messaging, raising privacy/GDPR issues.
  Source: https://www.spond.com/news-and-blog/volunteer-management-system/ (fetched, 2026-09-26; vendor blog)
- Small clubs often coordinate volunteers through a combination of Excel, WhatsApp, and "hoping for the best"; common hybrid: chat for urgent updates, a separate tool for scheduling and sign-up.
  Source: https://www.spond.com/news-and-blog/volunteer-management-system/ and https://www.volunteershiftmanager.com/learn/whatsapp-volunteer-communication (search results, 2026-09-26)

## Flat shares and households
- Cleaning in flat shares fails for three reasons: missing cleaning skills, rigid schedules that need someone to enforce them (the enforcer becomes the unwanted "cleaning police"), and perceived unfairness when residents spend unequal time at home. Suggested alternative: flexible status ("traffic light") plus transparent documentation so nobody has to police others.
  Source: https://liastoeffler.de/blog/3-gr%C3%BCnde-warum-putzen-in-der-wg-nicht-funktioniert (fetched, 2026-09-26)
- Flatmates not sticking to the cleaning schedule is a classic flat-share conflict.
  Source: https://www.fluter.de/wg-streit-mitbewohner-loesung and https://www.wiwi-treff.de/Wohnen-and-WG-Leben/Putzplan/Diskussion-29023 (search results, 2026-09-26; forum page returned HTTP 503 on fetch)
- Cognitive household labor (anticipating, identifying, deciding, monitoring) is unevenly split; mothers reported to handle 71 % of it.
  Source: https://remindher.app/best-task-sharing-apps-couples/ (search result, 2026-09-26; secondary source citing research, original study not checked)
