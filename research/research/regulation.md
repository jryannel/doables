# Research: Regulation (GDPR / DSGVO)

All accessed 2026-09-26. Not legal advice; secondary sources.

## Data minimisation and pseudonymous use
- Art. 5(1)(c) GDPR: personal data limited to what is necessary for the purpose. For many online services real names are not needed; pseudonymous or anonymous use should be examined and preferred. Pseudonymisation counts as data protection by design and as a technical and organizational measure under Art. 32(1)(a).
  Source: https://www.datenschutz.org/datenminimierung-dsgvo/ and https://www.thomashelbing.com/de/wissen/dsgvo-hub/einzelthemen/grundsaetze-der-verarbeitung/1.3.3.5-datenminimierung (search results, 2026-09-26)
- Relevance for Doables: a name plus sign-in key (no email) collects less personal data than account-based competitors. Whether customers perceive this as an advantage is untested.

## WhatsApp in clubs (Vereine)
- WhatsApp use for club communication falls under GDPR (phone numbers, group membership, and metadata are personal data). Blanket consent is insufficient; consent must be voluntary, informed, unambiguous, and revocable. Extra requirements for youth sections. Recommendation: real voluntariness, club devices instead of private address books, no sensitive data; dedicated club software allows communication "ohne private Telefonnummern".
  Source: https://klublink.de/whatsapp-im-verein-dsgvo.php (fetched, 2026-09-26; vendor page)
- Setting up WhatsApp groups by the club or club officials (e.g. trainers) is described as incompatible with GDPR; the club must not require WhatsApp and must not hand out contact data for group creation; only voluntary participation remains.
  Source: https://www.vibss.de/vereinsmanagement/marketing/social-media/whatsapp/whatsapp-rechtslage-mit-einfuehrung-der-eu-dsgvo (search result, 2026-09-26)
