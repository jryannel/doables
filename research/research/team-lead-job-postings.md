# Research: Team lead – job descriptions and planning practice

All accessed 2026-09-26. Job description templates and career guides, not individual postings (individual postings on StepStone/Indeed could not be fetched). Lower confidence than real postings.

- Team lead tasks: divide up work, delegate responsibilities, tasks, and working hours; organize regular meetings; take over weekly planning (Wochenplanung); focus on leading staff and monitoring projects and daily routines.
  Source: https://www.jobverde.de/karriereinfo/teamleitung-der-job-als-teamleiterin (search result, 2026-09-26)
- Team leads understand the project scope, break it down for the team, allocate resources, and ensure projects close within time and scope.
  Source: https://www.naukri.com/career-advice/team-leader-roles-and-responsibilities (search result, 2026-09-26)
- Team leads supervise day-to-day tasks, set goals and monitor individual and team progress, assist with workload planning and scheduling.
  Source: https://resources.workable.com/team-leader-job-description (search result, 2026-09-26)
- In agencies, account and project managers are responsible for resource allocation at account/project level.
  Source: https://www.operating.app/blog-posts/agency-resource-allocation-responsibilities (search result, 2026-09-26)
- Agency planning guidance: plan only about 80 % of capacity to leave room for the unexpected.
  Source: https://www.teamleader.eu/de/blog/ressourcenplanung-vorlage (search result, 2026-09-26)

## Trades (SMB) planning practice
- "Viele Betriebe planen noch immer im Kopf des Chefs, auf dem Whiteboard oder mit unzähligen WhatsApp-Nachrichten." From a team size of 5–6, planning by whiteboard or Excel becomes increasingly error-prone; smaller teams can manage with simple, visible approaches.
  Source: https://mein-handwerker-app.de/einsatzplanung-handwerk-tipps/ (fetched, 2026-09-26)
- Many trades businesses still plan staff "per Zuruf, Whiteboard oder Zettelwirtschaft".
  Source: https://bosch-officeon.com/de/de/wissen/blog/einsatzplanung-im-handwerk/ (search result, 2026-09-26)
