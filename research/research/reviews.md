# Research: Reviews (G2, Capterra)

All accessed 2026-09-26. Review pages were not opened directly (G2/Capterra block automated fetching); the statements below come from search result summaries of those pages. Treat as lower-confidence E1.

## Todoist
- Collaboration features are functional but limited for larger teams; limited collaboration controls and permission management for shared projects.
  Source: https://www.g2.com/products/todoist/reviews?page=8&qs=pros-and-cons and https://www.g2.com/products/todoist/reviews (search results, 2026-09-26)
- Team features described as "barebones": same task lists as personal use, plus assignment and comments.
  Source: https://thedigitalprojectmanager.com/tools/todoist-review/ (search result, 2026-09-26)

## Microsoft To Do
- Collaboration features feel limited; sharing lists is not always seamless when working with multiple people; praised as easy to use.
  Source: https://www.capterra.com/p/227173/Wunderlist/reviews/ (search result, 2026-09-26)

## Trello
- Free-plan limits (collaborator and board limits) described as hindering team collaboration; "paywalling" of basic collaborative features makes it less appealing for small teams. Cards and lists become overwhelming at volume; notification system confusing.
  Source: https://www.capterra.com/p/211559/Trello/reviews/ (search result, 2026-09-26)

## Asana
- A learning curve tops the complaints on G2; interface felt unintuitive; users report it takes time to understand workspaces, teams, projects, task hierarchies; "may be overkill" for very small teams.
  Source: https://www.g2.com/products/asana/reviews?qs=pros-and-cons , https://learn.g2.com/trello-vs-asana , https://www.smartsuite.com/blog/asana-review (search results, 2026-09-26)
