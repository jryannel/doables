# Intent

> Drafted by Claude from the live site (https://doables.gelbkappe.de) and the owner's one-line description ("a task list with sharing and team planning"). Target market, "why now", and success criteria are assumptions – please correct.

## Product idea (one sentence)
Small teams and groups that plan work together lose track of who does what across chat messages and ad-hoc lists; Doables gives them shared task lists with no sign-up friction (just a name, no password or email) so anyone can join a list in seconds and see what's due today and what's theirs.

## Target market
- Industry/industries: small teams without a dedicated project tool – agencies and freelancer collectives, small businesses (office, trades), clubs and associations, households and flat shares (assumption)
- Company size: 2–20 people per shared list (assumption)
- Region: DACH first, English-speaking users welcome (UI is English; assumption)

## Why now?
Team members already live in chat apps and are tired of heavyweight project tools (Asana, Jira, Monday) with accounts, seats, and onboarding. A zero-friction shared list that works on any device without registration fills the gap between a chat message and a project tool. (assumption)

## Success criteria
- A new user creates a first list and invites someone within 2 minutes.
- Shared lists are still in use after 4 weeks (weekly active lists).
- Invited people complete tasks without having to ask how the app works.
(assumption)

## Non-goals
- No full project management (Gantt charts, time tracking, budgets).
- No accounts with email/password; identity is a name plus a sign-in key.
- No enterprise administration.

## Known competitors and alternatives
Microsoft To Do (shared lists), Todoist (shared projects), Google Tasks, Apple Reminders (shared lists), Trello, Asana, Notion, WhatsApp/Signal group messages, paper lists and whiteboards, doing nothing.
